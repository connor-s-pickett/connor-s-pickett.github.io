About Me

I have been lucky enough to travel around the US and the world for my education. I obtained my Bachelors in Astronomy from Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University - Prescott in December 2021. In September 2022, I started my PhD at the University of Surrey and obtained my PhD in August 2026. Through this, I have found a passion for astrophysics and for the research necessary to unravel the mysteries of the Universe. I am currently applying to postdoctoral positions and intend to continue researching dark matter in Local Group dwarf galaxies.

Beyond academics, I enjoy spending time outdoors. I can frequently be found on hikes, taking photos of nature with cameras ranging from film to mirrorless. I have been playing lacrosse since 2015 and was able to continue doing so while completing my doctorate. I love to travel and have pursued the hobby throughout my studies. So far, I have ventured to 31 states and 8 countries. I am also an avid history buff, having obtained a minor in Diplomatic and Military History during my undergraduate studies.
