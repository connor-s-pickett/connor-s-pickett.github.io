PhD Research

Mass Modeling the Andromeda Dwarf Galaxies

My doctoral research involved the analysis of dwarf galaxies around our nearest galactic neighbor: Andromeda. These satellite galaxies tell us a story of dark matter, advancing our understanding of our model of the Universe.

Undergraduate Research

Spectral Analysis of eta Carinae

My undergraduate research focused on novel Sodium D absorption features in the massive binary star system, eta Carinae. My results revealed clues about an occulting debris cloud in the interstellar medium and highlighted shifting ionization effects of the system's surrounding Homunculus.
